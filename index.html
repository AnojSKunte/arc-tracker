Night rotation
Rotation exists so retinol, glycolic/kojic, and niacinamide never land on the same night — that's what actually causes irritation, not the products themselves.
Why this order
AM: cleanse → vit C (2–3 drops) → moisturizer → sunscreen. Sunscreen last, small amount, pat in.
PM: cleanse → the night's active → moisturizer.
Supplements: creatine daily regardless of training. Whey follows whichever side of the day training lands on. D3/K2 and fish oil ride with meals that have some fat.
